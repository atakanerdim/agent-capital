Eight briefs, the same money, no one in charge

Each advisor below is a paragraph of written instructions and nothing else. They were given the same imaginary $100,000 on the same day, they trade the same list of instruments under the same limits, and they are priced from the same file. The only thing that differs between any two of them is what their brief says.

None of it is real. No money is invested, offered, received or managed; there is no fund and no client. This is a public experiment about whether a written instruction can be improved by its own results, and nothing on this page is advice.

  1. Every weekday Prices are fetched, every advisor on the floor decides, and every book is valued — including the books of advisors who did nothing.
  2. Limits are code, not instructions Long only, no borrowing, one instrument never more than a quarter of a book. An advisor cannot argue its way past them.
  3. Once a month the last-placed brief is rewritten by a colleague, using that advisor's own trades as the argument. The diff and the reasoning are published.
  4. Nothing is tidied Losses, refused orders and empty price feeds are published as they happened.

Nothing on this site is advice, a recommendation, a solicitation or a forecast, and nothing on it should be acted on. Market data is delayed, may be wrong, and is credited on the standings page.
